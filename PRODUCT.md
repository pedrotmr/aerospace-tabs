# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People already using AeroSpace on macOS who want a quick, dependable way to scan and focus windows across workspaces. They use Aerospace Tabs alongside AeroSpace, not as a replacement for it.

## Product Purpose

Aerospace Tabs lists and focuses windows, keeps a local tab order, and provides a workspace-grouped window overview. AeroSpace remains responsible for tiling and layout. Success means users can identify and focus the intended window quickly without disrupting their existing workflow. After #8, focused and hovered tabs should be easy to distinguish at a glance, with no added focus latency or visual noise.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, native to macOS.

## Anti-references

- Zed-style switcher overlays.
- Wallpaper themes.
- Visual treatments that compete with the desktop or obscure AeroSpace's role.

## Design Principles

- Keep AeroSpace in control of tiling and layout.
- Make focused and hovered states clear without adding visual noise.
- Prefer macOS system appearance and semantic system colors over custom themes.
- Keep window focus immediate and predictable.
- Make keyboard paths clear and respect macOS accessibility settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keep focus and hover states readable and distinguishable; do not rely on color alone.
- Keep keyboard shortcuts and navigation discoverable and documented.
- Follow the active macOS appearance and system accent color.
- Respect Reduce Motion and avoid unnecessary motion in interaction paths.
- No formal WCAG conformance level has been specified.
